// Material breakdown

  • Engineered jacquard mesh. Variable weave zones — open where the foot needs air, dense where it needs hold.
  • TPU hot-melt overlays. Seamless support bonded to the upper instead of stitched onto it.
  • TPU midfoot cage. Integrated lockdown and lateral stability.
  • Supercritical EVA / PEBA. High-rebound foam for cushioning and energy return.
  • High-abrasion rubber outsole. Multi-directional traction for gym floors and outdoor runs.
