02 · Apparel

Tennis Collection

A three-layer bra — performance face, ventilation mesh, soft inner — over a skort with built-in shorts. Two colourways: the lime-to-cyan gradient, and white carrying fine lime and cyan lines.

What makes it different

  • Three-layer construction. Lightweight performance face, breathable mesh for ventilation, and a soft-touch inner against the skin.
  • Mesh racerback. Engineered openings across the shoulder blades, where heat builds during a match.
  • Built-in inner shorts. Coverage under the skort without a second garment.
  • Elastic comfort waistband. The FLOW logo sits on the band, secure without gripping.

04 · Footwear

FLOW R1

Engineered jacquard mesh over a supercritical EVA/PEBA midsole, locked down by a TPU midfoot cage. Drag across the shoe to spin it, or jump to the top and outsole views.

0°

Drag left / right to rotate

Material breakdown

  • Engineered jacquard mesh. Variable weave zones — open where the foot needs air, dense where it needs hold.
  • TPU hot-melt overlays. Seamless support bonded to the upper instead of stitched onto it.
  • TPU midfoot cage. Integrated lockdown and lateral stability.
  • Supercritical EVA / PEBA. High-rebound foam for cushioning and energy return.
  • High-abrasion rubber outsole. Multi-directional traction for gym floors and outdoor runs.
